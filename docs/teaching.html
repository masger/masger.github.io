Teaching & Mentorship

Teaching Experience

SEMS: Biostatistics, Epidemiology & R Programming

University of Copenhagen, Faculty of Health and Medical Sciences | 2021 to 2022

Instructor in biostatistics and epidemiology for 3rd semester MSc Medicine students (5 ECTS, ca. 20 students per class, 2 hours weekly). Directed practical case-based teaching covering hypothesis testing, linear and logistic regression, survival analysis, and statistical data analysis in R.

Clinical Pharmacology CRASH Course 1 & 2

Bispebjerg and Frederiksberg Hospital | 2021 to 2023

Co-organizer and instructor for acute hospital physicians and junior doctors (ca. 50 participants per course, held 4 times per semester). Covered applied pharmacokinetics, therapeutic drug monitoring (TDM), drug-drug interactions, and management of acute intoxications.

Danish Health Registers for Clinical Research

University of Copenhagen & Bispebjerg Hospital | 2021 to 2025

Annual lecture and practical seminar for clinical researchers and PhD students on using Danish nationwide health registries (LPR2, LPR3, Lægemiddelstatistikregisteret, CPR) for pharmacoepidemiology and observational research (ca. 20 participants).

Digital Health and Data (Module 1)

University of Copenhagen, Department of Computer Science | 2020 to 2021

Invited lecturer for postgraduate students and researchers on health data structures, electronic health records, and clinical data governance (ca. 50 participants per year).

Medical IT & Health Informatics

University of Copenhagen, Faculty of Health and Medical Sciences | 2008 to 2015

Lectures on medical IT systems, clinical databases, and informatics for 1st semester undergraduate medical students (ca. 150 to 200 students per semester).
